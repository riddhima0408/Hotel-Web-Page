Our Story

Rooted in Indian Tradition

Alankar Authentic Indian Hotel is a celebration of India's rich culinary heritage. From royal Mughlai dishes to traditional North Indian flavors, we bring timeless recipes to your table.

Our chefs use age-old cooking techniques, handpicked spices, and fresh ingredients to create dishes that reflect true Indian authenticity.

At Alankar, dining is not just about food — it's about culture, warmth, and unforgettable experiences shared with family and friends.
